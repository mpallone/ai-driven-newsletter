Fable 5 and Mythos 5 are offline globally — switch to Opus 4.8 now

  • Source: Anthropic (official statement, June 13, 2026); Simon Willison (simonwillison.net, June 13, 2026)
  • Link: Statement on the US government directive to suspend access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 — Anthropic
  • TL;DR: On June 12 at 5:21pm ET, Anthropic received a US government export control directive ordering them to stop providing Fable 5 and Mythos 5 to foreign nationals. Because Anthropic has no real-time way to verify user nationality at the request level, they disabled both models for every customer worldwide — including US users and Anthropic's own employees. The government's stated concern is a June 10 public jailbreak demo by "Pliny the Liberator," who claimed to extract functional instructions for cyber exploits from Fable 5. Anthropic says it reviewed the technique, found it narrow and comparable to known risks on other deployed models, and considers the directive a "misunderstanding" — but is complying. No restoration timeline has been given. Opus 4.8 and Sonnet 4.6 remain fully operational.
  • What to do: Replace any explicit claude-fable-5 or Mythos 5 model IDs in your configs with claude-opus-4-8 for long-horizon agentic work or claude-sonnet-4-6 for routine production traffic; if your Claude Code availableModels list includes only Fable 5 or Mythos 5, add claude-opus-4-8 now or the Default picker will fail to resolve.
  • Why trust it: Anthropic's official statement published June 13, 2026 (anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access); independently covered by Bloomberg, CNBC, Fortune, VentureBeat, Heise, and Al Jazeera on June 12–13, all citing the same directive and Anthropic's statement.
  • Skeptic check: Anthropic disputes the severity of the underlying jailbreak, and access could be restored quickly if the government accepts their explanation — but there is currently no confirmed timeline, and no workaround exists for US or international users while the models are offline.

Agent SDK billing split happens tomorrow — two steps required today

  • Source: Anthropic billing announcement (May 14, 2026); step-by-step breakdown by Codersera and ChatForest
  • Link: Anthropic Agent SDK Billing Split June 15 — ChatForest
  • TL;DR: Starting June 15 (tomorrow), claude -p, Claude Code GitHub Actions, Agent SDK calls, and third-party agents (OpenClaw, Conductor, Zed, Jean, and others) stop drawing from your Claude subscription usage quota and instead pull from a separate monthly credit pool: $20 for Pro, $100 for Max 5x, $200 for Max 20x, billed at full API list rates with no rollover. The credit is opt-in — if you haven't claimed it, your balance is $0 on June 15 and every automated request fails immediately, with no queue and no fallback.
  • What to do: Do both steps today: (1) Go to your Claude account settings and claim the Agent SDK credit — look for the Anthropic email sent around June 8 or the banner in account settings. (2) Enable the overflow billing toggle in the same settings panel so automated requests continue at API rates once the monthly credit is exhausted, rather than failing silently mid-run.
  • Why trust it: Anthropic's official announcement (May 14, 2026); credit amounts and the two-step opt-in requirement confirmed independently by Codersera, Apiyi.com, ChatForest, ByteIota, and DigitalApplied, all citing the same account-settings mechanics.
  • Skeptic check: The $20–$200 monthly credits sound substantial but are billed at full API list rates — a single long Claude Code automation session can burn through a $20 Pro credit in hours; model your expected token usage before assuming the credit covers your typical workload.
