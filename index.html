Production Codex runs are dominated by 1-hour+ tasks, not quick sprints

  • Source: OpenAI
  • Link: How agents are transforming work — OpenAI, June 28, 2026
  • TL;DR: First-party data from OpenAI's internal Codex deployment shows 80.6% of agent task requests now correspond to work that would take a person 30 or more minutes, and 70.2% correspond to an hour or more. This is not an edge case: Codex is the primary AI tool for every OpenAI department including Legal, Finance, and Recruiting, with non-developer users growing 189× since August 2025 and Codex now accounting for more than 85% of output tokens per average OpenAI employee. The median production run is a long-horizon task, not a quick code suggestion.
  • What to do: Plan your harness's context management strategy — compaction intervals, checkpoint frequency, session timeout thresholds, and cost estimates — around 45–90-minute runs as the normal case rather than 5–10 minutes.
  • Why trust it: First-party OpenAI data across their full internal Codex deployment spanning every department; includes longitudinal measurements from November 2025 through early June 2026; no independent external audit of the methodology.
  • Skeptic check: This is OpenAI's own workforce — the most enthusiastic and technically enabled Codex users anywhere. Adoption patterns and task duration distributions at external organizations will differ, possibly significantly.
